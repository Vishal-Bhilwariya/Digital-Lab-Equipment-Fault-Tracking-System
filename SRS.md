The Digital Lab Equipment Calibration & Fault Tracking System is a centralized web-based application designed to simplify laboratory equipment management in educational institutions. It provides three user roles: Administrator, Lab Technician, and Student, each with specific permissions.

The system enables administrators to manage laboratories and technicians, while lab technicians can register equipment, generate QR codes, maintain calibration records, and manage fault tickets. Students can scan QR codes to view equipment information, report faults with descriptions and photographs, and track ticket progress.

The application aims to replace manual registers and spreadsheets with organized digital records. Its key features include equipment management, QR-based identification, calibration tracking, fault reporting, repair management, and dashboards.

Built using React.js, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB, the system focuses on security, usability, reliability, and scalability while improving maintenance tracking and laboratory operational efficiency.
